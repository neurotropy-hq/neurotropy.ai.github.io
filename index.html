Our Vision

Your own brain is the missing variable in learning

We've all sat down to study and stared at the page. Not because the material was too hard, but because our brain simply wasn't ready yet. That can be pretty frustrating.

It turns out your capacity to learn depends on the state of your brain. Some brain states support learning, others don't. With real-time neurofeedback and AI personalized to your own brain, you can learn to shift into the right one yourself — session after session.

How it works

You're in control

Put on the headband

The EEG headband picks up your brain activity in real time.

Get live feedback

Our AI turns your brain activity into a simple visual signal, so you always know where you stand without us ever influencing your brain.

Guide the UFO yourself

In a gamified exercise, a small UFO drifts across the screen. By focusing on your brain state, you learn to guide it.
